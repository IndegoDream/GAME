# Phone (Android) rules -- speed and responsiveness first
Decided by Robert, 2026-10-21.
1. **Look:** the phone keeps the look it has now: the "low" quality tier in scripts/core/Gfx.gd (basic "kenney" art, Compatibility renderer, no SSAO/SSIL/SDFGI/volumetric fog, 2048 shadows, MSAA off). Do NOT give the phone the realistic/polyhaven/characters art or the high/ultra tiers. Seele's new visuals apply to PC; phone art stays basic so it never crashes or overheats.
2. **Everything else is shared:** every mechanics, balance, UI, control, menu, content, lore and bug-fix change applies to phone AND PC. Effects must have a cheap phone version (few GPUParticles, short lifetimes, no per-frame allocations, no extra full-screen passes; Settings > Effects Low is the phone default).
3. **Budget:** hold 60 fps on a mid phone (about 4 GB RAM, Snapdragon 6-series class); never let memory pass about 1.5 GB (the app was crashing on 2026-10-13 from memory, see MemLog). Frame time under 16 ms in the village and worst-case fights; first load under 10 s; menus open instantly.
4. **Responsiveness:** touch targets at least 48 dp, no input lag, no work on the main thread that takes over 4 ms (spread level building, pathfinding and saves over frames or threads), lazy-load art/audio, free what a floor no longer needs, cap enemies/projectiles/particles on screen, use MultiMesh/instancing, pool objects, texture compression ETC2/ASTC (already enabled), lower physics tick where the game allows.
5. **APK size:** keep the phone download small -- phone export should leave out PC-only high-res art (export filter / feature tags) so the installed game is lighter.
6. **Checks:** keep the existing gate green; add a phone-profile run (headless with the low tier) and report fps/memory for village, floor 1, floor 25 boss and floor 50.
